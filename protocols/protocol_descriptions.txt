Purchase:Facilitates buyer-seller-shipper coordination for product transactions and delivery. Does not accept credit cards.
Credit_Purchase:Same as Purchase, but adds a 5% surcharge for credit card transactions.
Logistics:Manages merchant-packer-labeler-wrapper workflow for order processing and fulfillment
Netbill:A two-party purchase protocol with time-deferred payment where the merchant delivers goods before the customer makes payment. Useful for urgent items with no concern for cost.
Flexible_Purchase:A two-party purchase protocol that supports multiple delivery options (standard and express) allowing agents to choose flexibility in service level.
